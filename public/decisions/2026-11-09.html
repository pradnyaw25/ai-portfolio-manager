Cash thesis

Cash at 29.4% is intentionally high because market breadth is weak, volatility has risen sharply, and the invested sleeve remains concentrated in a few large holdings and correlated technology exposures. The reserve reduces total-portfolio drawdown risk and preserves optionality to buy only after broader momentum stabilizes. It is not a signal to chase sharply falling candidates simply because they appear cheaper.
