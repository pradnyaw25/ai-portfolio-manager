Cash thesis

Cash at 27.3% is intentional risk control, not an absence of ideas. It offsets concentration in AAPL, JNJ, AMZN, and technology-linked positions while market breadth is weak and volatility is elevated. The reserve should not be deployed into falling semiconductor, AI-infrastructure, or watchlist names until there is evidence of stabilizing relative strength. It also preserves flexibility to add only if leadership broadens or high-quality holdings experience better-supported entry conditions.
