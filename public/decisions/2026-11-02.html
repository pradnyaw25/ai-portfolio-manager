Bear case response

Accept: INTC is the clearest current reduction candidate. It is down 43.3% over 30 days, 27.3% over five days, and 7.2% below cost; there is no supplied positive catalyst sufficient to justify retaining a small, deteriorating position. Accept: TSM, NVDA, AVGO, ORCL, GLW, META, GOOGL, and AMZN show broad weakness across growth, semiconductor, and AI-linked exposure. This supports avoiding additions and retaining cash, not indiscriminate liquidation of all holdings. Accept with qualification: GLW is down 18.7% over 30 days and has disclosed operating and customer risks, but it is slightly above cost, so its evidence supports a hold/watch stance rather than an automatic sale. Accept: QQQ's 6.3% 30-day decline versus SPY's 1.3% decline confirms an unfavorable near-term growth backdrop. Reject the implication that all portfolio risk assets should be sold: JNJ, MSFT, UNH, JPM, V, MA, PG, WMT, XOM, and HD have materially better relative strength, and wholesale selling would sacrifice diversification and realized leadership. Accept the risk view that strong unrealized gains are not a forward-looking risk control; therefore no new capital is allocated to payments, defensives, AAPL, or MSFT after their advances. The response is targeted de-risking in INTC and patience elsewhere.
