Bear case response

Accept the concern on GOOGL: it is down 9.0% versus cost and 9.8% over 30 days, so averaging down is not warranted without a demonstrated reversal. Accept that weakness is broader than the flat benchmark: AMZN, AVGO, CAT, GLW, META, NVDA, ORCL and TSM have all lagged materially, which argues against new cyclical or semiconductor exposure. Accept the specific GLW concern: its 20.0% 30-day decline and disclosed customer, financing, product, regulatory and demand-matching risks justify heightened monitoring, though its small weight and near-cost basis do not require a forced sale today. Accept the CAT, ORCL and AVGO momentum concerns; there is no evidence of stabilization sufficient to add. Accept rejection of sharply declining watchlist candidates including AMD, APLD, CRWV, IREN, INTC, SNDK and BE, as buying them would add demonstrated downside momentum. Accept that semiconductor weakness is clustered and therefore correlated, limiting the value of adding more chip exposure. Reject the implication that every winner should be reduced solely because other holdings are weak: MSFT, V, MA, XOM, WMT, PG and UNH have material relative strength versus SPY and support retaining, not expanding, existing exposure. The appropriate response is defensive selectivity, no averaging into weakness, and preservation of liquidity rather than broad liquidation.
