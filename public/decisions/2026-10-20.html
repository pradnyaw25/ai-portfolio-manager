Cash thesis

Cash is 28.1% of portfolio value, above the 25% threshold, and is intentionally retained. It offsets concentrated single-name and technology exposure, provides optionality if market breadth improves or quality holdings pull back, and prevents forced buying during a period when volatility is rising and many technology, semiconductor, infrastructure, and software candidates have weak momentum. The reserve is not a directional bet against equities; it is a risk-control allocation while existing core holdings already provide substantial market participation.
